# Before this becomes an official business site

This project is a functional demonstration. The form deliberately cannot deliver enquiries.

1. Obtain the owner's approval to use the site as the official business presence, and confirm the business name, phone number and location.
2. Confirm current services, travel areas, availability, site-visit arrangements and quotation terms. Approve or replace the proposed free-quote wording and proposed three-step journey.
3. For electrical, structural or other regulated work, confirm the work actually offered and any applicable qualifications, permissions and specialist involvement. Publish only evidence-backed claims.
4. Obtain suitable business/project images and permission to display them, including relevant photographer and customer permissions. Replace stock photographs and update labels accurately.
5. Connect a backend only after agreeing who receives enquiries, what information is necessary and how it is handled. Add server-side validation, abuse protection and an accurate privacy notice. Never put email-service secrets in browser code.
6. For photo uploads, implement server-side size/type/content validation, secure storage, restricted access and an agreed deletion policy. Do not convert the current local object URLs into public uploads.
7. Test actual delivery, errors and confirmations before changing the preview button into a live submission button. Remove demo messaging only when it matches the real behaviour.
8. Configure a domain and `SITE_URL` for accurate social metadata. Then review indexing, canonical URL, sitemap and verified structured business data. `noindex` and robots restrictions are intentionally enabled for the independent demo.
9. Review keyboard access, mobile layouts, image descriptions and content with the owner before launch.

No promises of reviews, prices, guarantees, insurance, accreditations or response times are included. Add them only with reliable supporting evidence and approval.
